Returns & FAQ

How ending, extending and returning a rental works.

How do I return a rental?

Open Orders, choose "Schedule return" on the item and pick a pickup date. Pickup is free, and monthly billing stops on the pickup date.

Can I return early?

Yes. You can schedule a pickup any time before your term ends and you only pay for the months you had the item (two weeks or less counts as half a month).

Can I keep something I'm renting?

Yes. Choose "Buy" on the Orders page to see your price: the buyout price minus half of the rent you've already paid.

Can I extend my rental?

Yes. Choose "Extend" on the Orders page and add 1, 3, 6 or 12 months at the same monthly price.

Can I exchange an item?

Schedule a return for the item you want to swap and rent the new piece. We'll pick up and deliver on the same visit when the dates match.

What if I bought an item and change my mind?

Purchases can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Schedule the pickup from the Orders page.

Can I cancel an order?

Yes, any time before the delivery date, from the Orders page.

What does delivery cost?

Delivery, setup and return pickup are free. Choose a delivery date and time window at checkout.
